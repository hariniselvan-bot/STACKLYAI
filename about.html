About Stackly

We exist to make intelligence inevitable.

Founded by engineers and strategists who believed enterprise AI should feel less like a science project and more like infrastructure.

Our story

From a two-person lab to 10,000 businesses.

Stackly began in 2019 with a single question: why does deploying one model take a year? Six years and 1,200 deployments later, the answer is our platform — strategy, build, integrate and optimize in one continuous motion.

Today we run intelligence for healthcare networks, global manufacturers and financial institutions across 40 countries.

Mission & vision

Why we show up.

Our Mission

To make enterprise intelligence as reliable, governable and beautiful as any piece of critical infrastructure — so every business can compound its decisions with AI.

Our Vision

A world where software doesn't just record work but does it — where every organization operates with the foresight of a thousand analysts and the speed of an agent.

Philosophy

Four beliefs, non-negotiable.

01 · Craft
Intelligence should be beautiful to operate. Polish is a feature, not a finish.
02 · Trust
No black boxes. Every decision your AI makes is explainable, auditable, reversible.
03 · Compounding
Each deployment should make the next one faster. Systems learn; so do we.
04 · Humanity
AI should elevate people — remove toil, amplify judgment, never replace accountability.
